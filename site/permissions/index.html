Permissions

Artifact Atlas reads nothing from your disk until you allow it. These are the four permissions involved, each with its own page.

  • Scan my home folder

    Required for real scansAsked inside Artifact Atlas

    The in-app consent that lets Artifact Atlas scan the data folders of the applications on your Mac.

  • Full Disk Access

    RecommendedmacOS privacy setting

    Why Artifact Atlas recommends macOS Full Disk Access and how to grant or remove it.

  • Data from other apps

    Asked by macOS when neededmacOS privacy prompt

    The macOS prompt that appears when Artifact Atlas reads another application's sandbox container.

  • Desktop, Documents and Downloads

    Asked by macOS when neededmacOS privacy prompt

    The macOS prompts for Desktop, Documents and Downloads, and when Artifact Atlas triggers them.

What Artifact Atlas does not ask for

  • No network access: nothing is uploaded and there is no account.
  • No administrator password and no system extensions.
  • No permission to control other apps. Applications you drop are read, never run.
